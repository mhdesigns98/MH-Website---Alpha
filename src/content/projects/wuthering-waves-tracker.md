## The Problem

Wuthering Waves has a deep farming system — materials drop on timers, routes need planning, and keeping track of what you need for each character upgrade gets complicated fast. Existing community spreadsheets were clunky and not personalized.

## What I Built

A local-first tool (no login, no server) that lets you:

- Track which resources you've farmed today and this week
- Plan optimal routes across zones
- Mark character upgrades and see exactly what materials are still needed
- Reset on the game's daily/weekly schedule automatically

## Why It Matters

This was a scratching-my-own-itch project that also served as a great exercise in building a data-driven tool with zero dependencies — just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Everything persists locally so it's fast and private.
