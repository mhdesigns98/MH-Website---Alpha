## The Work

VPM serves a large and growing audience across Virginia. The site needs to handle everything from breaking news to podcast pages to documentary microsites — all with a consistent visual language and a maintainable codebase.

My role involves:

- Designing and building reusable layout templates for different content types
- Creating a component library that keeps pages looking consistent
- Working with the editorial team to make sure the system is flexible enough for their needs
- Balancing visual identity with accessibility and performance

## Design Philosophy

Public media sites carry a lot of trust — people come for real journalism. The design has to get out of the way and let the content breathe, while still feeling modern and intentional.
