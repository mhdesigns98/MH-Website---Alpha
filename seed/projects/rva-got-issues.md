## The Show

RVA's Got Issues is a locally-rooted podcast from VPM that digs into the politics and personalities shaping Richmond. It has a distinct voice — direct, community-minded, and not afraid to go deep on local issues.

## The Site

The design needed to match that energy. The goals were:

- Make episodes easy to find and listen to
- Surface editorial content and show context
- Feel distinctly Richmond — not generic podcast template
- Work seamlessly on mobile (where most listeners find it)

## What I Focused On

Strong typography and a color palette rooted in the show's identity. Clean episode pages that lead with the content and make sharing easy. A homepage that gives first-time visitors enough context to know what the show is about immediately.
