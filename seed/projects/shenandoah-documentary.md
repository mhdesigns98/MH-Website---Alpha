## The Project

Documentary microsites are a different design challenge than editorial sites — they need to feel cinematic and immersive, not utilitarian. The Shenandoah site is designed to honor the visual work of the film while serving as a standalone digital experience.

## Design Approach

- Lead with visuals: full-bleed photography and video
- Typography that feels literary and crafted — not news-adjacent
- A structure that guides viewers through the story even if they haven't seen the film yet
- Clear pathways to watch, share, and learn more

## The Challenge

Balancing the rich visual identity of the documentary with the practical needs of a web audience — fast loading, accessible, and readable across all devices.
